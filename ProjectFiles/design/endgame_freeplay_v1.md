# Galeborne: Endgame, Free Play and Replay Variety (v1)

## 1. After Act 1: the Merchant Prince ladder
Five ranks, each with a visible goal and a reward you can see:
| Rank | Goal | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Trader | 10,000 coins net worth | 2nd ship, fleet orders unlocked |
| Merchant | Warehouses in 3 regions | Trade agents who auto-run one route |
| Magnate | Liberate 3 pirate ports | Your flag over those ports, tax income each moon |
| Sultan's Favourite | 25 ruler missions | A palace seat: you set one port's ban each moon |
| Prince of the Winds | Beat House Qarin, own a sky-ship | Your statue in your home port, free play unlocked on Hard |

## 2. Endgame loops that never run out
- Trade wars: a rival house moves into a region and undercuts you. Win it back by price, sabotage, or a fleet blockade.
- Pirate kings: a fixed roster of 8 legendary captains with lairs to raid. Each drops one legendary item.
- Grand contracts: huge time-limited orders (a sultan's wedding needs 500 silk in 3 moons) that need several ships and warehouses.
- The Wonders ledger: 12 long goals per region, like delivering every good or surviving a monsoon with a full hold.

## 3. Free Play mode
- Unlocks after Act 1 with any captain.
- Settings: difficulty (Relaxed, Captain, Hard), starting coins, pirate level, price volatility, number of rivals (0-2), with a hardcore option where a sinking ends the save.
- Optional win goal: reach a net worth target in a set number of years, scored on a local leaderboard.

## 4. Every save is different
- Building layouts: palace and bazaar fixed; the other buildings are rolled per save (the bank, moneylender, charm shop and black market only appear in some ports).
- Port leanings: each port's cheap and wanted goods shift by 1-2 goods per save.
- Event deck: each save draws its own sequence of shortages, festivals, wars and bans.
- Rival seed: the 2 active adversaries are drawn from the fixed roster, each with their own home port and methods.
- Captain stories: each captain has their own Act 1 with different opening ports, enemies and choices, so a second playthrough starts somewhere else.

## 5. The mid game: fleets
- Up to 5 ships. Each one can escort your flagship, run a trade route on its own, or patrol an area.
- Ships running routes on their own can be ambushed; you get a letter and can sail to help.

## Approved (Jeff, Oct 14) with these limits
- A ship can only automate a route the player has sailed by hand. Automated earnings run at 60% of a hand-sailed run, and offline earnings are capped at 8 hours.
- From Merchant rank on, ordinary pirates sail in packs of 2-4 that scale with your fleet, so the 2x flee rule still leaves fights common.
