# Free 3D Models Shortlist — Commercial Mobile (Galeborne)

Verified for **commercial use** only where the page states **CC0** or **CC-BY** (no NC, no ND).  
Unity 6.3 / Android — prefer **low-poly**; flag high-poly for decimation.

### Art lead note (textures)

Kenney / Quaternius **flat colours clash with our painted look**. For every entry:

- **UV / textures:** whether the mesh has proper UVs and a texture (or atlas) Chief Art Designer can **repaint**
- vs **vertex / flat materials only** (hard to match painted art without re-UVing)
- For **heroes and close-ups**, prefer **Sketchfab (or similar) models with realistic / hand-painted textures** under CC0/CC-BY — not flat Kenney/Quaternius characters
- **Heroes like Hasan** will likely still need **custom models** (Meshy or hand-built from paintings)

### Licence quick rules

| Licence | Commercial game OK? | Credit required? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CC0 | Yes | No (appreciated) |
| CC-BY | Yes | Yes |
| Quaternius QAL (newer packs) | Yes | No — do **not** redistribute as asset packs |
| Sketchfab Free Standard / NC / ND | **Do not use** for this list |

Re-check the live page before shipping; licences can change.
