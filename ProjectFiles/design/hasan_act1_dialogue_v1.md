# Hasan, Act 1 "Ashes on the Wind": dialogue v1
Mood tags: neutral / delighted / offended / suspicious. Lines are kept short for phone screens (at most 2 lines each).

## Beat 1: Prologue, the burning dhow (duel can't be lost)
- RAIDER (masked) [suspicious]: "Hasan Tazere. They said you'd burn slower."
- HASAN [offended]: "They said wrong. Get off my deck."
- (duel) If Hasan wins: RAIDER: "Keep your ship. You won't keep your luck." He leaps overboard.
- If Hasan loses: the crew drags the raider off and he flees. Same result.
- The raider drops a SCORCHED SEAL. HASAN [suspicious]: "A falcon in flames... House Qarin?"
- CHOICE 1 (raider in the water): Spare him (+Honor, he returns in Act 3) / Let him drown (+Fear, pirates hesitate later).

## Beat 2: Debal, Yusuf the old trader
- YUSUF [delighted]: "Hasan! You look like a kebab someone forgot."
- HASAN [neutral]: "Half my cargo's ash. I need coin, fast."
- YUSUF [neutral]: "Then listen. Buy where it's piled, sell where they're begging. The sea does the rest."
- (market tutorial) After the first profit: YUSUF [delighted]: "See? Even a burnt man can be clever."

## Beat 3: The seal
- YUSUF [suspicious]: "Put that away. Qarin's seal opens doors... and graves."
- YUSUF: "They're buying every warehouse from Gwadar to Muscat. Nobody knows why."
- STORY GOAL: "Find out what House Qarin is hoarding."

## Beat 4: The Sultan of Muscat's peacock
- SULTAN [delighted]: "A trader with soot on his face! Perfect. Bring me a white peacock in two moons."
- HASAN [neutral]: "Majesty, peacocks don't really... travel."
- SULTAN [offended]: "Then teach it. Succeed, and my customs men will look the other way. Once."
- Reward: a one-time customs pass + Sultan standing.
- CHOICE 3 (later): Warn the Sultan about Qarin's agents in his court (+Sultan, Qarin turns hostile sooner) / Stay quiet (Qarin offers a bribe).

## Beat 5: Farida's rugs run
- FARIDA [suspicious]: "Rugs. Muscat's banned them, so Muscat pays triple. Interested?"
- HASAN [neutral]: "And if customs finds them?"
- FARIDA [delighted]: "Then they're very nice rugs you bought for your mother."
- CHOICE 2: Run the rugs (Farida becomes an ally, her papers get cheaper) / Betray her to customs (+Sultan, 500 coins, she becomes a rival smuggler).

## Beat 6: Ambush near Gwadar
- LOOKOUT [suspicious]: "Captain! Sails out of the fog, and they're flying no flag!"
- After the fight: a dying pirate presses prayer BEADS into Hasan's hand. "Omar... said give these... to his brother."
- HASAN [offended]: "Omar? My brother's been dead five years."
- STORY GOAL: "Find out who sent Omar's beads."

## Beat 7: The Qarin warehouse, and the sky
- (Gwadar, night) Inside the Qarin warehouse: no goods, only balloon silk, hydrogen jars and charts with routes over mountains.
- HASAN [suspicious]: "They're not trading by sea. They're building ships that fly."
- Outside, a Qarin airship rises through the storm. A face in its gondola: OMAR.
- OMAR [neutral]: "Go home, brother. The sea is finished."
- HASAN raises the old compass. Its needle swings... upward.
- HASAN [delighted]: "Then I'll follow you into the sky."
- END OF ACT 1. Unlocks: Sassi and Jiand, the sky-route goal, the Silk Road region (roadmap).
