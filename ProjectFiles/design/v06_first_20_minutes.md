# First 20 minutes, v2 (Oct 15). This version replaces everything below it.
1. 0:00 The prologue duel at the burning dhow. You can't lose it, and the raider drops the Qarin seal.
2. 2:00 Gwadar at dawn. Tap around the town and buy dates and salt. There are no ads, and the warehouse and tithe are hidden.
3. 6:00 Sail to Debal. Meet Yusuf, sell your cargo, and hear the gunpowder rumor in the tavern.
4. 9:00 Debal's back alley. Farida sells gunpowder at Gwadar's price, but only during the opening. It goes in your hold.
5. 11:00 The voyage to Muscat. The fog ambush hits on voyage 3, and if you lose, the gunpowder is spared.
6. 14:00 Muscat. You always clear customs on the first run, and the banned sale pays about 2.9× (copper at 2× if you skipped the gunpowder).
7. 16:00 After the sale, the warehouse, the temple tithe, the bank and the lender unlock.
8. Voyage 4 onward: real customs risk and opt-in ads begin. After the opening, gunpowder is only sold in Gwadar.

# v0.6 Gwadar slice: the first 20 minutes (beat by beat)

Goal: a new player lands a 3x jackpot within 20 minutes, and always has a short, medium and long goal on screen.

| Min | Beat | What the player learns | Goals on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | Hasan docks at Gwadar at dawn. Camera rises over the tall town. Tap the bazaar. | Tapping buildings, meeting the keepers | Short: buy cheap. Medium: lease a warehouse. Long: find who burned your ship (Qarin seal) |
| 2-5 | Buy dates and salt cheap (one tap, slider). | Buying, the hold, cheap goods | |
| 5-6 | Set Sail on the chart table, to Debal (safe route). No pirates (pirate-free voyages 1-2). | The map, routes, danger level | |
| 6-9 | Debal: sell for about 1.5x. Tavern rumor: "Muscat will ban gunpowder next moon." | Selling, rumors | Short changes to: buy gunpowder |
| 9-11 | Back in Gwadar: lease a warehouse, buy gunpowder from Farida in the back alley, store it. Farida throws in forged papers (first run only), so customs risk shows as about 15-20%. | Warehouses, contraband, heat | |
| 11-13 | Temple tithe (optional, the customs hint is shown). Sail to Muscat on voyage 3; the first ambush can trigger (fog). | Customs risk, ambushes | |
| 13-16 | Fog ambush with an early pirate. Tutorial prompts for chain shot at the sails, then grape at the crew; it surrenders or you board. | Aiming, ammo, ways to win | |
| 16-19 | Muscat: the ban is live. Customs check in slow motion, the stamp, you're through. Sell at 2x+: coins spill, the merchant gasps. About 3x profit on the run. | The jackpot | Medium done. New medium: buy a bigger ship. |
| 19-20 | A letter with the Qarin seal is found in the pirate's strongbox (only if you boarded) or in a rumor (if not). | Story hook | Long goal advances |

Tuning rules
- Rumor and ban are scripted on the first playthrough; random afterwards.
- If the player skips the gunpowder, the rumor repeats once, then a forced copper shortage in Muscat gives a backup 2x.
- A loss in the ambush costs 30% of cargo; gunpowder is spared on the first run so the jackpot still lands.
- Caught at customs on the first run: the gunpowder is confiscated, coins are kept.
- Banned goods in Muscat sell at 3x with a 1% drop per unit (Jeff, Oct 14).
