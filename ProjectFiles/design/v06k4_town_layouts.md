# k4 Town Layouts — Debal, Muscat, Gwadar
Each town = one silhouette from the water + a different tap-around walk. Reuse terrace modules but change arrangement, height and color. Landmark sits at the far edge, ~1/3 previous scale, never at sea or in fights.

## Gwadar — low fishing harbour (home port)
- Shape: flat, wide crescent quay along a sandy beach; one cliff on the LEFT.
- Terraces: 1 level only, low mud-brick boxes, spread out with gaps.
- Landmark: squat sandstone watchtower on the left cliff; small dome mosque center-back.
- Props: net racks, beached boats, fish baskets along the quay.
- Buildings (left→right): Harbour master, Shipwright (repair only, −20%), Market, Tavern, Mosque/Temple.
- Palette: mud brick, faded blue doors.
- Mood: quiet, morning light.

## Debal — dense trade city (smuggling hub)
- Shape: busy stepped city climbing a slope behind a long wooden pier.
- Terraces: 3 stacked levels, tightly packed, narrow lanes between.
- Landmark: tall stepped temple spire with RED pennant at top-RIGHT; teak-balconied customs house on the pier.
- Props: market awnings, rug stalls, crates, crowd (KayKit, distance only).
- Buildings (bottom→top): Customs house + pier, Market, Shipwright (sells small hulls — "coming soon"), Tavern, back-alley entrance (Farida), Temple at top.
- Palette: sandstone, saffron/red awnings.
- Mood: dusk, oil lamps.

## Muscat — fortified royal port (court & customs)
- Shape: horseshoe cove enclosed by dark rocky crags on both sides.
- Terraces: 2 levels of whitewashed blocks hugging the cove, with flat roofs.
- Landmark: round white crenellated fort on the RIGHT crag; gatehouse below it leading to the palace.
- Props: guards at the gate, customs tables at the dock, palms.
- Buildings: Harbour (no shipwright — "Muscat Harbour"), Customs, Market, Tavern, Palace (via gatehouse), Temple, back alley (own painting).
- Palette: whitewash, coral stone, dark rock, brass.
- Mood: bright midday.
