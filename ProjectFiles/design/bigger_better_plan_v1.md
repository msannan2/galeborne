# Galeborne: Bigger and Better (v1, awaiting Sannan's approval)

| System | Original | Galeborne goes bigger | The "one more voyage" moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market | Buy low, sell high, every port lists every good | ~30 goods; prices react to events (famine, war, festivals, a sultan's wedding); boom-and-bust waves you can ride | A rumor says Muscat's harvest failed. You fill your hold with grain, race a rival there, and sell at 4x |
| Contraband | Banned goods sell at 2x, with customs risk | Bans shift every moon; heat builds per port; forged papers, bribes and hidden holds; customs chases at sea | You slip a hold of gunpowder past a slow-motion customs stamp and triple your money |
| Warehouses | Store goods | Lease or buy in every region; stockpile before price spikes; rivals can raid or burn them, you can guard them | You've hoarded silk for 3 moons, the ban drops, and you sell the lot in one spike |
| Charms & curses | Magic shop charms, some bad omens | 40+ charms; curses with a visible tell; a travelling charm caravan; the Enchantress lifts curses for a price | A cursed charm you nearly bought turns out to be what sinks your rival |
| Combat | Simple broadsides | Wind, aimed shots at sails, guns, hull or crew, four ammo types, boarding duels, ship abilities | You chain-shot a pirate's sails, board, win the duel, and take a legendary cannon |
| Ports | One painted scene with buildings to tap | 3D tall towns, unique keepers, special buildings in some ports, liberated ports change visibly | You burn the pirate flags over Gwadar and the town's colors and new shops return |
| Ships | A handful | 20+ ships across dhows, junks, galleons and sky-ships; 3 upgrade tiers; regional shipwright specialties; fleets with orders | A launch scene at the yard for your first galleon, then it sails into your home harbor |
| Captains | Several with backstories | 6 at launch, each with their own Act 1, a unique ability, and personal rivals | Sassi's story unlocks and you replay the map with a whole new playstyle |
| Adversaries | Pirates | Named rivals with memory who undercut you, frame you, raid you; capped at 2 active | After 3 moons of sabotage you bankrupt House Qarin's agent in his own port |
| Sultans | Ruler missions | 150+ missions; sultans remember favors and grudges; crazy requests with humor | The Sultan of Muscat wants a live peacock delivered in 2 weeks, and you somehow do it |
| Money | Bank and money lender | Bank interest and safety; lender loans with debt collectors; a trading empire you can watch grow | You borrow big, land one jackpot, and repay it all in a single run |

**Three goals always on screen:** this run (a sale target), the next step (a ship, port or warehouse), and the long goal (Omar and the sky routes).

**Every jackpot gets a spectacle:** coins spill across the ledger, a stunned merchant, a ship launch, flags burning in a liberated port, a fleet that shows up on the map.
