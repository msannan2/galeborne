# Hasan Tazere — Act 1: "Ashes on the Wind" (about the first hour; for Sannan's approval)

## Premise
Hasan's family home in the desert outside Debal is burned in a single night. His brother Omar is gone. Among the ashes Hasan finds a brass sky-compass that points up, not north, and a scorched merchant's seal.

## Beats (each beat teaches one system)
1. **Prologue: The Burning (tutorial, 2 min).** Short playable scene: Hasan duels a masked raider in the courtyard (teaches the boarding duel). Raiders flee by sea. Hasan keeps the sky-compass.
2. **Debal Harbor (trading tutorial).** Old family friend, harbormaster Yusuf, sells Hasan a battered dhow on credit. First trade run: cotton to Muscat. Goal: first profit within 5 minutes.
3. **The Scorched Seal (port and palace).** In Muscat, the seal belongs to the House of Qarin, a respected rival trading house. The Sultan of Muscat, vain and theatrical, will only talk if Hasan finds his runaway peacock (first funny mission).
4. **Whispers in the Tavern (rumors and contraband).** A smuggler named Farida says Omar was seen chained aboard a black-sailed ship heading for Gwadar. She'll guide Hasan if he runs one cargo of rugs past customs (teaches contraband and customs risk; she lends a Faker's Crate).
5. **First Blood (combat).** The black-sailed ship's escort ambushes Hasan near Gwadar. First real battle. Board the escort to find Omar's broken prayer beads and a manifest: "cargo: one scholar, deliver to the sky."
6. **The Warehouse in Gwadar (warehouses).** The manifest leads to a Qarin warehouse. Hasan leases the one next door to watch it, then sneaks in at night: crates of sky-crystals, contraband everywhere.
7. **Act Ending: The Ship That Flew.** From Gwadar's cliffs Hasan sees the black-sailed ship rise out of the sea and vanish into the clouds. The sky-compass glows. Hook: Hasan needs a ship that can fly.

## Act 1 choices (small but remembered)
- Spare or kill the masked raider in the prologue (he returns later as an enemy or an informant).
- Honor Farida's deal or betray her to customs for a reward (she becomes ally or rival).
- Tell the Sultan of Muscat about the House of Qarin now or keep quiet (affects Act 2 politics).

## Characters introduced
- **Omar:** Hasan's younger brother, a scholar of old star charts. Why he was taken is the main mystery.
- **Yusuf:** harbormaster and mentor, warm and funny.
- **Farida:** sharp-tongued smuggler, possible romance or rival.
- **The Sultan of Muscat:** vain, comic, secretly clever.
- **House of Qarin:** the first rival trading house; their masked master is Hasan's nemesis.

## Ties to the main mystery
Omar can read the old sky-charts, and someone needs him to find the collapsed sky-routes. The sky-compass is a key to them.
